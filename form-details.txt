PROJECT DETAILS FOR GOOGLE FORM

Project Name:
   AI Student Notes Summarizer & Quiz Generator

Project Description:
   An AI-powered web application designed for students to instantly summarize lengthy study materials, lecture notes, and textbook content into clear, actionable bullet points, while automatically generating quick practice quizzes to test comprehension.

Project Statement:
   Students often face information overload with massive syllabi and lengthy notes, making rapid revision difficult during exams. Traditional note-taking and manual flashcard creation are time-consuming, leading to inefficient learning.

Your Solution:
   Our solution leverages Gemini AI to parse long paragraphs and extract core key concepts in under 3 seconds. It formats the summary into easy-to-read bullet points and dynamically generates a 3-question multiple-choice quiz so students can immediately test their understanding.

Uniqueness & Innovation:
   Unlike basic summarizers, this tool combines instant summarization with automated quiz generation in a clean, distraction-free, single-page interface. It requires zero setup and works directly in the browser with high speed and zero clutter.

Technology Stack:
   HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+), Google Gemini API
